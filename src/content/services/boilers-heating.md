A boiler that's serviced every year runs more efficiently, breaks down less and keeps its warranty valid. If it has already stopped working, we carry common parts on the van so most repairs are done in a single visit.

## Annual service

We clean and inspect the burner and heat exchanger, test combustion and flue gases, check pressure, valves and seals, and confirm the carbon monoxide alarm works. You get a written service record.

## Common repairs

- No heat or no hot water
- Boiler losing pressure
- Radiators cold at the top or bottom
- Noisy pumps, banging pipes or short cycling

## Better control

A modern thermostat with room-by-room zones can stop you heating empty rooms. We install and set it up, then show you how to use the schedule.
