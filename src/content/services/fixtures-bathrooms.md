We fit faucets, toilets, showers and sinks, from a single dripping tap to a full bathroom refit. Every joint is leak-tested before we leave, and we tell you how long the silicone needs before the shower can be used.

## What we install

- Kitchen and bathroom faucets
- Toilets, including low-flow and wall-hung
- Shower valves, mixers and screens
- Sinks, vanities and garbage disposals
- Dishwasher, washer and fridge water lines

## Bathroom refits

For larger projects we plan the plumbing with you first, including where the pipes run, what can move and what it costs. We work alongside your tiler or bring a trusted one, and keep the house usable while we work.

## Bring your own fixtures

If you've already bought a faucet or toilet, we'll fit it. Send us the model number before the visit and we'll check that it suits your pipework and water pressure.
