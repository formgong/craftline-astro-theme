Most slow drains are grease, hair or wipes. On older lots it's often tree roots in the sewer line. We clear the blockage and find out which it was, so you're not calling us again next month.

## What we clear

- Kitchen sinks and garbage disposals
- Showers, tubs and bathroom basins
- Toilets and floor drains
- Main sewer lines and outdoor drains

## Camera inspection

On main lines we run a waterproof camera through the pipe after clearing it and show you the footage on a tablet. Roots, a sagging section and cracked joints are easy to spot. If the pipe needs repair, we give you a written price and you can take a few days to decide.

## Keeping drains clear

Use a strainer in showers, keep grease and wipes out of the drains, and run hot water after using the disposal. Once a year, a maintenance flush on older sewer lines costs far less than an emergency call.
